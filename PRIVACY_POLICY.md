### 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how **Soft Splits** ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. **Soft Splits is designed as an offline training tool and does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal user data.**

---

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).
- **Financial Information:** Soft Splits is strictly an educational and practice trainer. No real money, deposits, purchases, or financial transactions take place within the App.
- **Location Information:** We do not access, track, or store your GPS or network location.
- **Device & Usage Data:** We do not collect device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, MAC address), hardware details, or usage logs.
- **Biometric or Sensor Data:** We do not access or collect any camera, microphone, or sensor data.

---

### 3. Local Data Storage
Any gameplay progress, training statistics, or user preferences (such as sound toggle or table settings) are stored locally on your device using standard Android local storage. This data never leaves your device and is permanently removed if you uninstall or clear the data for the App.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services and Analytics
Soft Splits does not incorporate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics or Facebook SDK). We do not share any data with third parties.

---

### 5. Internet & Device Permissions
Soft Splits requires **no sensitive runtime permissions** (such as Camera, Contacts, Microphone, Storage, or Location).

### 6. Children's Privacy
Soft Splits does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age in your jurisdiction). The App does not facilitate real-money gambling.
